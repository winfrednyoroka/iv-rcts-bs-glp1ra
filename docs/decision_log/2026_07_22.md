- Mapping evidence outcomes, use diagonal lines between 60 and 120 months to emphasise broken axis.
- Exclude Schiavon et al 2019, the reported BP is average change in 24 h ambulatory blood pressure and BMI mean is not reported.
- Primary (1o) analysis
    - BMI measured at 12 months, BP traits at 24 months
- Secondary analysis
    - BMI and BP trait measured at 12 months
    - BMI and BP trait measured at 24 months


## GLP1RAs
- Mapping evidence outcomes, show there are no repeat measures of either BMI or BP for the same study across different time points.
- Primary (1o) analysis
    - BMI measured and BP traits at 6 months
- Secondary analysis
    - BMI and BP trait measured at 12 through 24 months
